# Ubiquitous language

- **Authorized reviewer**: a person or independent service whose public signing
  key belongs to the review group declared by the pinned policy.
- **Approver**: the named group member allowed to authorize a transition after
  inspecting the review. Ordinary group membership alone confers no approval.
- **Transition intent**: the exact Run, current State, proposed successor,
  selected input, current work digest, and transition sequence being approved.
- **Review policy**: the pinned group membership, approver, required review count,
  public verification material, and verifier identity. It contains no private key.
- **WRA**: Wishlist Review Authorization, this issue's requirement prefix.
