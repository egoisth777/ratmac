# Ticket: t-069 — Child as reviewer

## Vertical Outcome

Judge independence lands child-as-reviewer first. A parent standing at a branching Phase spawns a
declared reviewer child; the child Run performs the review and its Engine writes its durable
terminal fact; the judgment reaches the parent as ordinary transition-input delivery (FDC-003
verdict) authored inside the child's turn - no human courier between machines. No witnessed
verdict verb exists in the surface; its deferral is recorded, not silently dropped: signer
identity stays outside the Engine (ORS-001).

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** `src/scheduler.rs` the reviewer flow needs no new verb - the proof is the
  composition of spawn (t-065), the ledger (t-066), and verdict delivery (FDC-003); this turn
  wires and proves it end-to-end and closes any seam that blocks a child-authored verdict from
  landing in the parent's admission surface, `test/qa/tests/t069_child_reviewer.rs`,
  `.arca-private/t-069/` hidden lanes, `.arca/runbook-authoring.md` reviewer-pattern note.
- **Excluded:** any witnessed-verdict or signing verb, signer identity, quorum, group membership
  (all explicitly deferred - ORS-001), the cycle runbook.
- **Expected merge/output:** the parent's branch consumes a child-authored verdict exactly as it
  consumes any valid verdict: strict validation, monotonic evidence archive, then State File
  advance; provenance adds no new trust machinery this increment.
- **Superseded-test fate:** none expected - existing verdict oracles (strict validation, archive
  before advance) must hold unchanged for child-authored bytes.
