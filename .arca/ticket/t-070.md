# Ticket: t-070 — Full doctor executable fingerprint

## Vertical Outcome

Argument-free `rtm doctor` exposes the complete SHA-256 identity of the exact Engine it is
already running: 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters in the human environment report, with no
write or behavioral boundary moved.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** a fresh public red and final oracle in
  `test/qa/tests/t045_bootstrap_doctor.rs`; the one-line human-renderer change in
  `src/cli.rs::environment_report`; inherited `t045_bootstrap_doctor` and
  `t057_deep_doctor` behavior; public workspace, formatting, and warning-denying Clippy checks;
  and one hidden lane per category under `.arca-private/t-070/`.
- **Excluded:** changes to executable selection or SHA-256 computation; pin or trust behavior;
  runtime-state reporting; Runbook findings; `--json` or arbitrary-path behavior; and every
  archived trial byte.
- **Expected merge/output:** the existing hash of the exact resolved Engine renders in full in
  the argument-free human report. All inherited doctor/bootstrap boundaries stay unchanged.
- **Superseded-test fate:** none. The focused regression adds the previously absent full-digest
  oracle; inherited doctor and bootstrap coverage remains authoritative.
