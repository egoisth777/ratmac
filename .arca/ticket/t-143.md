# Ticket: t-143 — case-scoped test hooks

## Vertical Outcome

A test hook that one test case arms through process-wide environment settings (`RATMAC_TEST_*`, such as the Engine's hold test barrier) reaches only that case's own commands. No other case running alongside, in the same test process or another, has its `rtm hold` or any other command wait on the hook, time out on it, or otherwise observe it. This holds in the public suites under `test/qa` and in the declared, non-expired private lanes under `test-hidden`. The arming case still meets its hook, including today's expiry refusal, whose reason reads `hold test barrier expired before <boundary> waiting for <release file>`. A check proves deterministically that an `rtm hold` on another fixture, run while a barrier-armed `rtm hold` is parked, never sees the barrier.

## Worktree Scope

Implement only [the accepted case-scoped hold barrier issue](../issue/i-068-case-scoped-hold-barrier/spec.md).

A private lane under `test-hidden/` changes only where the inventory check (`SVEV-018`) finds a process-wide arming outside the accepted scopes. The rule-fix recovery and authorized review transitions suites stay serialized under the hook lane; `hook_lane`, `scrub`, `isolated_test`, `StepFault`, and `HookLane` in `test/qa/src/support.rs` keep their behavior, `HookLane` gains no refusal of its own, and the Engine under `src/` stays unchanged. The checks learn that an armed hold is parked from its marker's whole boundary line, through the shared whole-line wait of the whole pause marker line requirement (SVE-005). Add only the directly required module or command wiring where the accepted design calls for it; keep unrelated behavior unchanged.
