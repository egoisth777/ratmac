# Ticket: t-047 — Implementation-completion gate verifies work

## Vertical Outcome

Before a ticket may be `passed` and its residuals `satisfied`, the gate runs or verifies fresh receipts for the ticket's focused tests, affected regressions, applicable hidden lanes, and required quality gates, refusing receiptless relabeling by naming the first missing receipt.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the completion predicate, receipt freshness and re-derivation checks, and its refusal path.
- **Excluded:** receipt authoring (t-045) and routing changes (t-048).
- **Expected merge/output:** a completion gate that cannot be satisfied by relabeling.
