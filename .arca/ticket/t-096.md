# Ticket: t-096 — The edition sequence has no holes and never moves

## Vertical Outcome

A citation to an edition keeps resolving. The audit reports, by name, a gap in the edition sequence
and an edition whose commit is no longer the one it marked, so a moved or deleted edition is caught
by a check instead of by a broken citation months later.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the sequence and immutability half of the audit, extending
  `test/qa/tests/t095_edition_audit.rs`, plus six private lane checks. Immutability is proven the
  only way a repository can prove it: every edition's recorded commit is compared against a
  committed record of what it marked, so moving a tag becomes a reported difference rather than an
  invisible edit.
- **Why now:** the property is worthless once relied upon and unchecked; the first citation to
  `edition-001` in a gap record is already in the tree.
- **Excluded:** no history rewriting is prevented by this ticket - version control cannot be made
  to refuse it here; the audit only makes it visible. No edition is created or moved by any test.
- **Expected merge/output:** the audit passes on this repository, and fails by name on a missing
  number in the sequence or on an edition pointing somewhere new.
- **Superseded-test fate:** none superseded; `t-095`'s audit gains checks rather than being
  rewritten.
