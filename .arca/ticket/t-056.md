# Ticket: t-056 — Deep doctor: findings, codes, exit codes

## Vertical Outcome

`rtm doctor` diagnoses through the t-055 parser and produces one list of findings — stable code, severity, location, message — from four passes: parse and schema, graph, guard lint, and the PGE-004 ownership audit. `--json` emits that list; the human report renders the same list. `rtm doctor <path>` diagnoses any runbook file, inside or outside the project, and writes nothing; argument-free `rtm doctor` keeps its ORS-002 environment report and appends the findings. Exit is `0` clean, `1` warnings only, `2` on any error.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** a new `src/doctor.rs` (finding model, code table, graph checks, guard lint, ownership wiring), `src/cli.rs` (argument parsing for `<path>` and `--json`, exit-code plumbing), `src/bin/rtm.rs` (process exit code), and the diagnostics section of `.arca/runbook-spec.md`.
- **Excluded:** scaffolding and authoring guidance (t-057). The doctor remains read-only; no guard is executed to diagnose it.
- **Expected merge/output:** a code-carrying, machine-readable, exit-code-differentiated doctor with no reader of its own.
