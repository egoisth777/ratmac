# Ticket: t-064 — Composition format extension

## Vertical Outcome

One runbook can declare a composed machine. The format authority and the typed parse gain the
class and spawn tables and the `join` guard kind: a runbook carrying them parses without the
format-restriction refusal and comes back doctor-clean; a malformed declaration refuses with a
stable RB5xx code naming the offending key; `blocked-route` stays the canonical hyphen spelling.
Declarations are inert - no runtime behavior changes, nothing spawns.

## P5 notes (2026-08-03)

**What landed.** `MachineClass` now parses the three composition surfaces FDC-009 declares and
nothing routes through them: an inline `classes` table of whole child machines (each body reuses
`parse_machine_body`, the same phases/transitions rules as the top level with every location
prefixed `class "<name>" `), per-Phase `spawns` arrays naming a declared class, a Phase-unique
child, and a bind list, and the `join` guard kind (`require = "all_passed"`, optional `min >= 1`).
Static validation proves spawn/class coherence at parse (RB504 undeclared class, RB505 binding-set
mismatch); shape defects are RB501/RB502/RB503 and join-vocabulary defects RB506. The format is one
level deep by construction: a class body accepts no `classes` and its Phases no `spawns` - both
refuse as RB103 unknown keys, the shape FDC-012 caps. `Scheduler::evaluate_join` refuses honestly
while no spawn ledger records children (FDC-011 owns the ledger, t-066). The spec's new "Classes
and spawns" section is the format authority; the authoring instructions gained the six repair rows
and a linked Composing walkthrough; the t-057 catalogue and t-058 drill police RB501-RB506.

**Failure and interruption review.** A refused parse and a doctor diagnosis write nothing
(HT-064-04 byte-compares the tree). Declarations are dormant: start/step on a composed runbook
match the tableless twin exactly (HT-064-03), and the pinned-read drift refusal is unchanged
(HT-064-06). A join guard cannot be satisfied this increment - the honest refusal names 0 recorded
children - so no composed machine can leak through a join before the ledger exists.
