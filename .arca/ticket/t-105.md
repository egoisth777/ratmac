# Ticket: t-105 - A checker learns a ticket's checks from its tags, never its prose

## Vertical Outcome

A workflow-side checker in the qa crate reads a ticket's three front-matter tag
lists - `focused-tests`, `hidden-lanes`, `quality-commands` - as its complete
declared-check set, and a malformed list refuses naming the field and the entry.
Stripping every prose section from a tagged ticket leaves the checker's answer
unchanged. No Engine source changes: the completion gate's cutover stays the
deferred `CGD-003`.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** a `ratmac_qa` tag reader (parse the three lists from ticket front
  matter; report the declared set; named refusals for a non-list, an empty string,
  and a cross-field duplicate); public tests proving prose-independence over this
  sprint's own tagged tickets (`GPH-003`: this repository is the growing fixture);
  six hidden lane checks.
- **Excluded:** any `src/` edit - the Engine keeps parsing prose until `CGD-003`
  is selected; any rewrite of archived tickets, which are judged by the rules they
  were cut under.
- **Why now:** the tag format landed at P1; without a reader it is prose with
  extra steps - the checker is what makes the tags load-bearing.
