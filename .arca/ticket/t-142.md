# Ticket: t-142 — whole pause marker line

## Vertical Outcome

Every test that waits on a pause marker, in the public suites under `test/qa` and in the declared, non-expired private lanes under `test-hidden`, acts only on the marker's whole boundary line: its complete text, ending in its newline. A missing file, an empty read, or text without its closing newline counts as not yet published: the test keeps waiting until its time limit and never compares, parses, or proceeds on such a read, nor on the file merely existing. A wait therefore fails only when the whole line never arrives in time or arrives with other text, so a pause-seam test never fails on timing alone. A check stages empty and partial markers deterministically and proves every waiter holds until the line is whole.
