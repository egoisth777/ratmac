# t-030 — Document Main-Agent step policy

## Vertical Outcome

The CLI help and executable workflow documentation state that only the Main-Agent or human invokes `schd step`, while Subagents only read state and never invoke `schd`; this remains documented rather than caller-authenticated, consistent with ADR-0003.

## Worktree Scope

**Included:** Add the policy to the user-facing `schd step`/workflow help surface and test that the documented Main-Agent/human versus Subagent rule is explicit.

**Excluded:** Caller authentication or identity enforcement (intentionally not code-enforced in ADR-0003), Exit Guard semantics, and the user-only `schd start` policy owned by t-029.

**Expected merge/output:** A focused help/documentation change with an executable CLI/help proof for the documented step policy, without a fake caller-identity gate.
