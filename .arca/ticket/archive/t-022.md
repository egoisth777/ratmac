# t-022 — Append transition records to the log

## Vertical Outcome

A successful `schd step` appends exactly one human-readable transition record to `.arca/log.md` and never rewrites or removes earlier records.

## Worktree Scope

**Included:** Implement Scheduler-owned append-only Transition Log writes and the two requirement-specific public checks for transition append and preservation of existing bytes.

**Excluded:** Guard evaluation, state schema, lock arbitration, and prompt rendering owned by other tickets.

**Expected merge/output:** A focused Rust log writer integrated with the successful transition path from t-016, plus deterministic tests proving one append and preservation of prior log content.
