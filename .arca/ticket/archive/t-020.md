# Ticket: t-020 — Actionable guard refusal report

## Vertical Outcome
A refused `schd step` report identifies the failing guard and states the observed fact alongside the expected fact, giving the caller a concrete correction signal.

## Worktree Scope
- **Included:** Deterministic refusal-report construction with guard identity and observed-versus-expected fact fields.
- **Excluded:** Guard evaluation (t-017), refusal state transaction (t-018), and blocked-status classification (t-019).
- **Expected merge/output:** Report formatting and `T-03` test merge after t-018; every guard failure exposes actionable fact evidence without mutating state.
