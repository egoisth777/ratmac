# Ticket: t-050 — Trial lifecycle: deterministic identity, atomic start, dry-run

## Vertical Outcome

`tools/trial.ps1 start` creates `trial-<nnn>-<slug>` at the clean experiment base tip with its linked sibling worktree, atomically or not at all; every dirty or colliding precondition refuses with a named reason and zero mutation; identity is inferred deterministically across branches, tags, and durable directories; `status` previews plans and recovery commands while mutating nothing.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the script skeleton with `start` and `status`, preflights, identity derivation, rollback, and the plan/recovery printer.
- **Excluded:** finish, sync, and the interface audit (t-051, t-052).
- **Expected merge/output:** a reversible, previewable way to open a trial.
