# Ticket: t-006 — Restrict blocked to Scheduler entry prerequisites

## Vertical Outcome

When a required entry prerequisite is absent, the Scheduler records `status = blocked` and names that prerequisite in `blocker`; `blocked` never becomes a Phase or machine-graph state.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** Scheduler-owned entry-prerequisite evaluation, blocked status assignment, and the blocker field/report needed to distinguish it from machine state.
- **Excluded:** the Phase-only model (t-001), general status enum (t-002), Exit Guard evaluation/refusal (t-016/t-018/t-019), and persistence layout.
- **Expected merge/output:** missing entry prerequisites yield an explicit blocked status and blocker fact without adding a graph state; goal `T-06` is green.
