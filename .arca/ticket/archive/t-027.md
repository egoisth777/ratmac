# t-027 — Keep TOML comments out of prompts

## Vertical Outcome

Comments in a human-authored `ratmac.toml` are parser-discarded phase-intent notes for reviewers and never appear in the Phase Prompt printed to an agent.

## Worktree Scope

**Included:** Treat TOML comments as parser-discarded, non-output intent and add the owned prompt-output exclusion test.

**Expected merge/output:** A focused strict-parser/rendering integration and fixture test that proves comment text cannot leak into agent-visible output.
