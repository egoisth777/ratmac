# Ticket: t-151 — bounded lane-report publication

## Vertical Outcome

The sweep still publishes exactly one complete verdict per crate and a total by replacing its complete same-directory temporary report in one atomic move. On Windows, an access-denied replacement (`PermissionError` with `winerror == 5`) waits and retries for at most five seconds measured from the first denial, at the existing ten-millisecond retry interval. A successful retry publishes the computed report without rerunning any crate. A lasting denial raises the last error and leaves the old report whole; the existing cleanup removes the temporary file. No retry starts at or after the deadline. Every other error, and every non-Windows replacement failure, keeps its single-try behavior.

This repairs the observed LNR-001 publication failure. The holder and the duration of the original denial are unknown. It does not change the verdict rules, expiry policy, close guard, frozen goal, or archived issue.
