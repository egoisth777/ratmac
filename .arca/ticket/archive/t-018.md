# Ticket: t-018 — Refuse and stay on guard failure

## Vertical Outcome
A failing Exit Guard makes `schd step` refuse with a report and leave Phase and Status unchanged, with no retry counter and no Transition Log entry beyond the refusal report.

## Worktree Scope
- **Included:** Failure transaction boundary for `schd step`, including refusal report emission and suppression of state/counter/transition-log mutation.
- **Excluded:** Artifact fact evaluation (t-017), blocked-status distinction (t-019), report fact wording (t-020), and successful transition behavior.
- **Expected merge/output:** Refusal handling and `T-01`/`T-02` tests merge after t-017; repeated failure remains a no-mutation operation.
