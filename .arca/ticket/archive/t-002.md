# Ticket: t-002 — Keep status phase-local

## Vertical Outcome

A Run records `status` as one of `planned|executing|blocked|passed|failed` alongside its current Phase, while transition definitions and graph traversal remain independent of that status value.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the Scheduler status enum and its separation from the Phase graph, including validation of the five lifecycle values.
- **Excluded:** Phase-only graph construction (t-001), blocked prerequisite assignment (t-006), state-file persistence (t-007), and CLI behavior.
- **Expected merge/output:** status is represented and validated as phase-local lifecycle data, with `PT-002-01` green and t-001 behavior preserved.
