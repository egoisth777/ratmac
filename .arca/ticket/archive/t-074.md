# Ticket: t-074 — Child workspace binding

## Vertical Outcome

A spawn may bind its child Run to an explicit workspace. `rtm spawn --workspace <path>`
canonicalizes that path and records it in the Scheduler-owned child-ledger entry; omitting
it records the parent workspace. Future child guards and motion resolve against the stored
binding, not against whichever directory a later caller happened to use.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** `spawn --workspace` argument handling; canonicalization and refusal of an
  unusable binding; ledger schema/write/read updates that populate the existing workspace
  field without weakening append/annotate ownership; parent-workspace inheritance; child
  guard and motion workspace resolution; sibling receipt paths; QA coverage in
  `test/qa/tests/t074_child_workspace.rs`; and six private lane checks.
- **Excluded:** repository-wide root and mint ownership (`t-071` and `t-072`), lock
  mechanics (`t-073`), child-as-reviewer semantics, join policy, and any multi-level
  spawning expansion. This ticket does not let agents edit a child ledger directly.
- **Expected merge/output:** the ledger is the durable source of a child workspace binding;
  subsequent child operations use exactly its canonical path, and parallel sibling child
  receipts are tracked below separate `.ratmac/evidence/<run-id>/` paths.
- **Superseded-test fate:** tests that only establish the reserved, always-empty ledger
  workspace slot are replaced with populated explicit and inherited binding assertions;
  existing append-only, abandonment-mark, respawn, and missing-child ledger tests remain.

## P5 Review Notes

- **Inherited respawn locking finding (not changed by t-074):** a successor can be
  admitted before it is recorded in its parent's spawn ledger. The red baseline
  `01decae:src/scheduler.rs:1224-1288` already minted, dropped
  `successor_run_lock`, retired the predecessor, and only then appended the
  successor entry. The same independent sequence remains at current
  `src/scheduler.rs:1411-1473`; t-074's workspace validation occurs before mint
  and its workspace clone changes no lock or interleaving semantics. A concurrent
  abandon can therefore retire that unrecorded successor. This is inherited
  respawn-locking debt, outside the child-workspace binding scope; it is recorded
  here for landing follow-up rather than silently fixed by this ticket.
- **Workspace pathname identity policy:** a durable child binding is its canonical pathname, so
  deleting and recreating a directory at exactly that pathname retains the binding, while a missing
  path refuses and the recreated path's guards decide its contents without any fallback elsewhere.
- **Independent review.** `t074-review` rejected the first finished tree with three findings: an
  abandoned ledger record could fall back to the caller's directory, a recorded workspace could be
  redirected underneath the child, and the respawn successor interval. The first two were fixed and
  cleared; the third was proved inherited and is recorded above. The final verdict is APPROVE.
- **Change.** `src/cli.rs` parses `--workspace`, `src/scheduler.rs` canonicalizes, records,
  inherits, and resolves the binding, and `src/receipt.rs` scopes receipt paths by Run id. A live
  defect - overlapping sibling children resolving the first matching child class instead of their
  own - was caught by `HT-074-06`, not by the public plan; the public tests were left as declared.
- **Reviewable snapshot.** Cited by `res-111` and `res-103`, not here: those records name the
  manifest path, its row count, and its one declared exception.
