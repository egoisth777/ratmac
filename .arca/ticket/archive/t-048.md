# Ticket: t-048 — Human-authorized blocked route

## Vertical Outcome

A human may `hold` an executing ticket that carries a `blocker-ref` to a new five-file issue or named residual; the Run then routes to intake or planning while the ticket stays not-passed and its residuals unproven. Without authorization or without a blocker link the route refuses, leaving Scheduler-owned state and history unchanged.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the `held` ticket state, the `blocker-ref` field in `.arca/tpl/ticket.md`, the `p5-blocked` route predicate, and its refusals.
- **Excluded:** abandonment (t-049) and completion (t-047).
- **Expected merge/output:** an honest exit for out-of-scope blockage.
