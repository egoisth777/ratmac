# Ticket: t-046 — Intake and record contract gates

## Vertical Outcome

The intake gate verifies that every issue ends integrated or rejected with its five-file shape, that every accepted requirement ID exists in the goal, and that links resolve; the record gate verifies one residual per frozen requirement citing the frozen revision, evidence behind every `satisfied`, single-ticket ownership of every gap, acyclic dependencies, and complete ticket sections.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** existing intake and record predicates plus `src/contract.rs` enumeration of both
  `.arca/residual/*.md` and `.arca/residual/archive/*.md`, exact comparison with every requirement
  ID in the frozen `.arca/goal/spec.md`, duplicate detection across both locations, and fixtures
  for missing, archived, and duplicate-across-folders records.
- **Excluded:** completion gate (t-047), blocked route (t-048), and residual creation/movement.
- **Expected merge/output:** the record gate proves a total one-to-one mapping; absence can never
  satisfy it and archive is durable presence, not discarded history.
