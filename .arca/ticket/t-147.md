# Ticket: t-147 — unreadable record refusal

## Vertical Outcome

When `python tools/verify_issue_binding.py check` meets the item record the turn is bound to, or a gap record that item lists, and that record exists but cannot be opened or read, it refuses with exactly one line on standard error in its usual refusal form: `verify_issue_binding: cannot read the item record <path>: <reason>` or `verify_issue_binding: cannot read the residual record <path>: <reason>`. `<path>` is shown as the neighbouring `does not exist` rows show it, with forward slashes, and `<reason>` is the operating system's reason text; this ticket settles the choice the issue left open as the error's short reason, not its full text. The validator exits 1, prints nothing on standard output, writes nothing, and never prints a Python traceback. A record path that is absent or is a folder keeps today's `does not exist` refusal, and every other row keeps its behavior. The validator copy packaged in the plan-build profile behaves identically and stays byte-identical to the tool.
