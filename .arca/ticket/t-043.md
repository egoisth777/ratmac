# Ticket: t-043 — One caller policy on every active surface

## Vertical Outcome

Every active caller-facing surface — `rtm start` help, `AGENTS.md`, `.arca/index.md`, and any canonical skill present — states the one policy (a human may invoke argument-free `rtm start`; the Main-Agent only after explicit human Run-start sign-off; a Subagent never invokes `rtm`), an executable audit fails on any retired user-only or blanket never-agent-start wording, and the Engine gains no caller identity, token, or authorization state.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the policy text on each active surface, retirement of the superseded R-007 wording in tests, the stale-policy audit with its seeded-fixture negative, and the no-caller-state check.
- **Excluded:** bootstrap, doctor, and pin comparison (t-053); behavioral role scenarios (t-044).
- **Expected merge/output:** one policy, stated once per surface and mechanically defended.
