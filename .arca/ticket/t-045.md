# Ticket: t-045 — Evidence receipts and agent-writable evidence ownership

## Vertical Outcome

`.arca/evidence/` is the agent-writable receipt location with a structured per-check schema (command, working directory, target refs, exit status, output digest); the P4-equivalent gate accepts only per-planned-test sensitivity receipts, refusing prose and filename conventions; and an executable audit proves no active prompt or gate contract instructs writing a Scheduler-owned file.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the receipt schema and writer, the sensitivity gate predicate, the evidence-location documentation, and the prompt/gate ownership audit.
- **Excluded:** the completion gate (t-047) and intake/record gates (t-046).
- **Expected merge/output:** receipts that later gates consume instead of prose.
