# Ticket: t-051 — Trial lifecycle: validated log, ordered finish, containment

## Vertical Outcome

A committed `trial-log.md` is mechanically validated for required non-empty sections and identity consistency; `finish` runs from a clean base checkout in fixed order — verified annotated archive tag, durable log commit, worktree removal, tag-verified branch deletion — refusing the failing step and all later steps; the base gains exactly the durable log and the deleted branch is recoverable from its tag.

## Worktree Scope

- **Included:** the log template and validator, the finish sequence with per-step refusals and resume output, and containment/recovery proofs.
- **Excluded:** sync and the interface audit (t-052).
- **Expected merge/output:** trials that end reversibly with a durable record.
