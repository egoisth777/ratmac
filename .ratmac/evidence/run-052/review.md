Accepted on 2026-10-03, before executing the final full workspace proof.

- Before the contract went in, it was refreshed against the landed `main`: the draft had been written before `t-123` through `t-129` landed. The refresh corrected the decision-trace paragraph (a refused held step emits one refusal-code record of the new failure kind `held`, under `t-128`'s per-failure rule) and the cross-references to `t-129`'s canonical intent. Before red, the test writers' questions were settled in the ticket, never by the implementer: the held-step refusal renders `observed`; a replayed resolution names the sequence it was already consumed at; every suite composes the routed class itself from the recorded base runbook bytes; doctor's same-State warning `RB207` narrows to non-blocked transitions; `RB901` is named in the `journal/` prose row and comes from the Run scan alone; and hidden `ht_114_06` resumes its held child through the library pair before stepping it.

- Each review took two rounds. In the first, the source review asked for one high and four medium repairs, plus one low: `rtm abandon` (and `respawn`'s supersede path) retired a Run without first finishing a committed pause entry, leaving it pending forever; the outside-every-declared-root hold refusal had gained quotes around the blocker, moving today's bytes; recovery skipped the noncanonical-occurrence refusal; resume never served the `before-state-write` barrier; `rtm intent --resume --review` bound a review-set digest, so its render was not the canonical resolution intent; and a `t-129` archived-record refusal text had changed.
